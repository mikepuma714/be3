How It Works

Simple. Transparent. Agent-First.

Here's exactly what happens from contract to commission check using a representative 85% split example.

1
You Close A Deal

Congratulations! Your client buys or sells a home. Let's say it's a $500,000 property with a 3% buyer's agent commission — that's $15,000 in gross commission.

2
Your Commission Is Split

Using an example 85% split: your gross share is $15,000 × 85% = $12,750. The Century 21 franchise fee (8%) is shared proportionally to your split — already factored in, so you net approximately $11,730 on this deal.

3
You Get Paid

Your commission is processed through Dotloop. Our admin team handles the paperwork. You receive your check. That's it. No waiting, no confusion.

4
And The Cycle Repeats

Because you're getting daily training and daily marketing support, the next deal is already in the pipeline. This is how our agents average a deal a month.

Note on the franchise fee: The 8% Century 21 franchise fee is split in proportion to your commission tier. At 85/15, you contribute 85% of the 8% and we cover the remaining 15%. This is already reflected in the net figures above and in the calculator below.
