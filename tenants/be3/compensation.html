Complete Fee Structure

Every Number. No Surprises.

Full transparency — here is every fee you will ever pay at BE3, exactly as it is.

$149
One-Time Startup Fee

Charged once when you join. Covers CRM setup, custom website, email, and full Beggins University access. Never charged again.

$149/mo
Monthly Membership Fee

Covers all technology, daily training, support staff, and Regional President mentorship. MLS dues are paid separately to your local MLS board. Pensacola: $189/mo — same plan with MLS dues bundled in.

$299/deal
Transaction Fee

Charged per closed transaction. Includes E&O (Errors & Omissions) insurance for that deal. No separate E&O bill.

$0
Franchise Fee to Agent

We pay the Century 21 franchise fee entirely on our side. Agents at other C21 offices pay 6–8% of gross commission. At BE3 that is zero.

How It Works

Simple. Transparent. Agent-First.

Here's exactly what happens from contract to commission check.

1
You Close A Deal

Congratulations! Your client buys or sells a home. Let's say it's a $500,000 property with a 3% buyer's agent commission — that's $15,000 in gross commission.

2
Your Commission Is Split

At BE3, you keep 90% = $13,500. The brokerage retains 10% = $1,500. No franchise fee deducted from your side. No surprise fees. Clear and simple every time.

3
You Get Paid

Your commission is processed through Dotloop. Our admin team handles the paperwork. You receive your check. That's it. No waiting, no confusion.

4
And The Cycle Repeats

Because you're getting daily training and daily marketing support, the next deal is already in the pipeline. This is how our agents average a deal a month.
