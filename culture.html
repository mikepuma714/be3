Our Story

30+ Years of Building Something Different

Century 21 BE3 wasn't built in a boardroom. It was built in the field, transaction by transaction, agent by agent, over three decades of serving Florida's families and empowering its real estate professionals.

We started with a simple belief: if you take care of your agents, your agents will take care of your clients. Everything we've built — the training, the tools, the culture, the support — flows from that idea.

Today, we're the #1 Century 21 in Florida (by more than double the next closest) and #12 in the entire United States. But the number we're most proud of? The 60,000+ families our agents have helped buy and sell a home.

Local Leadership

Become A Regional President

Our Regional President role is one of the most unique and rewarding opportunities in the brokerage world. Are you a top producer who also loves developing other agents?

Despite being cloud-based, we have a Regional President in every market we serve. The RP is the in-market, human layer of coaching and support that makes our cloud model work — and keeps our culture intact across every region.

Regional Presidents are verified top producers who love the teaching side of the business. In addition to maintaining their own production, they mentor, coach, and hold accountable the agents in their region.

  • Earn override income on your region's production
  • Build your own team within the BE3 structure
  • Full corporate support and marketing for your region
  • Mentorship from company leadership and fellow RPs
  • Be a part of something bigger than just your desk

About Leadership

Built By Three. Driven By Purpose.

After 30 years of leading the charge with continual innovation — from founding the Century 21 brand in Florida back in the ’70s, to building one of the largest C21’s in the U.S. & the world — we’ve realized there are two types of agents. Those who appreciate the hands-on connectivity that a brick and mortar traditional office brings, and then there are those who appreciate the flexibility and freedom that comes from a connected, cloud-based experience. We decided to offer both.

We are on a mission to create the coolest, most agent-focused and robust brokerage model ever created. Through economies of scale, experience, strategy and leveraging technology to lower our overhead, we can now offer our cloud-based agents the powerful tools of a leading global brand — Century 21. We’ve coupled that with a 90% net split and no franchise fees.

When you combine world class training, the best global branding, true connectivity with support to match, you have a simply unrivaled combination. You deserve it. Let’s go.
